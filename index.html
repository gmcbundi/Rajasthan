Rajasthan Medical College Support & Complaint Portal

Our Aim: To ensure all Government and Private Medical Colleges across Rajasthan remain strictly ragging-free and uphold the highest standards of safety, ethics, and education. This centralized portal empowers students, staff, and citizens to seamlessly submit any complaints, queries, or grievances regarding medical colleges directly to the National Medical Commission (NMC) and the Government of Rajasthan.

Help & General Complaints

For any general assistance, inquiries, or official complaints in Rajasthan, please reach out to our support team. We aim to respond promptly.

help.kdec@gmail.com help@rajasthan.jo3.org

Anti-Ragging Cell

We maintain a strict zero-tolerance policy towards ragging. If you face or witness any form of ragging, please report it immediately. Your identity will be kept confidential.

antiragging@rajasthan.jo3.org
